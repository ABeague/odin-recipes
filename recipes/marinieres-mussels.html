Marinières Style Mussels

A classical seafood dish bringing back memories of seaside holidays. Perfect with french fries on the side.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg mussels
  • 2 chalots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 a 20 g bunch of parsley
  • 30 g salted butter
  • 30 cl dry white wine
  • thyme

Steps

  1. Clean mussels.
  2. Chop chalots and garlic, then parsley. Put on the side.
  3. Melt butter in a pot, add chalots then garlic. Cook on low heat.
  4. Add mussels, white wine, thyme, parsley. Stir, cover and cook.
  5. While stirring, raise the bottom mussels to let the top ones cook. Leave for 10 minutes. When all mussels are opened, tey are cooked.
