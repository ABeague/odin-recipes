Lemon Pie

This delicious, melts-in-your-mouth lemon meringue pie has its sour touch softened by the creme fraiche. With its light and creamy meringue, it's a delight for Mother's Day... or for dessert or snacking on a sunday.

Ingredients

Shortcrust pastry

  • 250 g flour
  • 125 g butter
  • 70 g sugar
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 5 cl water
  • A pinch salt

Lemon cream

  • 2 lemons
  • 180 g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 20 cl creme fraiche
  • A pinch salt

Meringue

  • 4 egg whites
  • 150 g powdered sugar

Steps

Making the shortcrust

  1. Preheat oven to 180 °C.
  2. Beat egg yolks and sugar with water until pale. Keep egg whites on the side.
  3. Mix with hands flour and butter cut into small pieces until firm like sand.
  4. Pour liquid mixture in the middle. Make a ball with the palm of your hands and knead once or twice.
  5. Spread dough on baking paper. Poke holes over the bottom with a fork. Cover with baking paper and dry beans. Bake for 10 minutes (the dough must not brown or only very slighly).

Making the lemon cream

  1. In a bowl, mix 2 whole eggs and 2 yolks (keep whites on the side), creme fraiche and sugar.
  2. Add juice of both lemons and their very finely grated zest.
  3. Remove dough from oven. Remove beans and baking paper and pour in the cream. Bake for 30 minutes.

Making the meringue

Whip the 4 egg whites. When stiff, slowly add powdered sugar while whipping.

Putting it all together

Once the pie has baked, spread meringue on cream and bake for 10 more minutes until meringue browns. Leave in turned off oven during 40 minutes for a crunchy meringue.
